Vnezapno Fact publisher

Vnezapno Fact («Внезапно факт», “Suddenly a Fact”) is a channel of short fact-checked educational videos in Russian, under 60 seconds each, published on YouTube, Telegram, VK and TikTok.

Vnezapno Fact publisher is the channel creator's own internal tool. It prepares one video per day and, after the creator approves it manually, uploads it to the creator's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. Nothing is published without the creator's approval.

The tool is not offered to anyone else, has no other users and no public sign-in. It uses YouTube API Services; see the Privacy Policy.
